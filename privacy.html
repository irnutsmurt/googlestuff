Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This app lets a self-hosted Home Assistant instance access the Google account that authorizes it. Google data is used only to provide Home Assistant features, stays on that private server, and is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by removing the integration from Home Assistant.

Contact: open an issue on GitHub.
