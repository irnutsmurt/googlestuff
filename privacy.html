Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how googlestuff ("the app") handles information when you authorize it to access your Google account.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only the OAuth scopes needed for the scripts you run. Depending on those scripts, this may include data from Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, or your basic Google profile (name and email address).

How information is used

  • Data is used solely to perform the actions you request when running the app.
  • Data is processed locally on the computer where the app runs.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never shared.

Sharing and disclosure

The app does not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google user data to any third party. It does not use your data for advertising, and it does not use your data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep copies of your Google data beyond what is needed to complete a requested action. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token file also removes the app's access on that computer.

Security

Credentials and tokens are kept on the local machine and transmitted only to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on the GitHub repository.
