googlestuff

googlestuff is a set of personal automation scripts that use Google APIs (such as Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive) to help the owner manage their own Google account data.

The application is operated by an individual for personal use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered for sale.

What it does

  • Signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 so the scripts can act only on the account that granted access.
  • Reads and organizes data in the user's own Google account according to the scripts the user runs.
  • Runs locally on the user's own computer; no data is sent to third-party servers.
